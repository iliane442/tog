Alabasta · La route des terrasses
Génération des cinq planches source avec l’outil de génération d’images intégré.
Référence visuelle utilisée pour chaque planche : maquette A validée dans la conversation.
Les planches ont été découpées en sprites. Les textures de sol ont été mises au format 128 × 128, puis projetées sur une grille isométrique 2:1.
Les rendus ont été assemblés avec les PNG du kit.

PORTE DES CARAVANES DE NANOHANA

Sprite individuel créé avec l’outil de génération d’images intégré. Le gabarit suit la projection 2:1 de l’enceinte. La seconde référence est porte_royale.png.

Habille le gabarit de l’image 1 en pixel art pour la porte des caravanes de Nanohana. Calque exactement sa silhouette, les pieds des tours, la diagonale des corniches et la hauteur de l’arche. Garde la tour de droite nettement plus haut dans l’image que celle de gauche. Reprends la pierre miel, les dômes dorés et les bannières bleues au soleil de l’image 2. Passage ouvert et transparent, fond transparent, sans sol, personnages ni texte.

NANOHANA

Use case: stylized-concept. Deliverable: transparent isolated production sprite atlas for a real pixel-art RPG. The reference is the accepted Alabasta terraced map concept, demonstrably reference for art direction only. Create sharp retro 32-bit pixel art, deliberate staircase pixels with clustered shading. Fixed orthographic 2:1 isometric camera with ground axes rising one for two horizontal, same orientation across absolutely all sprites, like a tile/sprite RPG. Ivory honey sandstone masonry, light lit from the upper left; tightly limited colors per material, absolutely no blur, anti-aliased appearance, ambient FX, or text labels. Each sprite's structural footprint is flat and visibly tilts with the isometric grid. No large ground bases, isolated object trimmed to silhouette, and no shadows outside the solid sprite. Crisp readable shapes and game proportions. 
LAYOUT IS CRITICAL: Exactly six isolated objects. Generate a 3-column by 2-row contact sheet, 3:2 aspect ratio if possible, six exactly equal square cells with no drawn grid, no panel borders, no extras and no backgrounds. Each object centered horizontally and vertically within its own cell with large transparent margins on all four sides. Objects never cross cell borders. Do not redesign the layout, do not rearrange the objects. Each object occupies 70–80% of the cell's width except tall narrow objects. Render on genuine alpha transparency.

Row 2, left: one wooden market stall, striped red and saffron canvas awning, small fruit and crates under it. TERRAIN

Use case: stylized-concept. Production pixel-art paving and terrain texture swatch atlas, reference image for colors and material motifs in a vintage sunny desert RPG. Output a single wide flat rectangular 4-column by 2-row atlas, preferably 2:1 aspect ratio, EXACTLY eight equal edge-to-edge square material regions at exact geometric grid boundaries. Every region filled entirely with its material from edge to edge and should look seamless and uniform. No gaps, framing, labels, text, borders, gradients, shadows or objects. Camera for the TEXTURE ONLY is perfectly perpendicular orthographic top down, no projection, do NOT draw diamond cutouts or terrain platforms. Keep all details in crisp, large clusters suitable for downsampling into 128px textures; sandstone and plank strokes exactly horizontal and vertical. Upper row, left to right: slot (1) warm sunny honey ochre desert sand, subtle fine ripple strokes and few ochre speckles; slot (2) large buff ivory and peach sandstone square flagstones, subtle thin brown seams, matte; slot (3) weathered warm teak wooden deck planks (straight parallel boards, very few nails, no edge of deck); slot (4) saturated vivid lapis/turquoise open sea water with small flat cyan pixel strokes and an occasional white glint uniformly spaced. Lower row, left to right: slot (5) heavily banded dry orange-red sedimentary rock exposed cliff face, ochre highlights and sparse brown vertical fissures, viewed as flat face to be projected later; slot (6) warm pale cream ashlar stone retaining wall with staggered bonded rectangular bricks and fine brown mortar, flat texture; slot (7) dusty light peach-packed caravan path, a few tiny flat stones and cream grit; slot (8) green-blue shaded ornamental shallow canal water with gentle sparse reflections, no lotus, no shore. Important: no grid lines, only obvious color and motif changes mark the eight cells.
