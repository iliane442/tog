# Décors d’Orange Town

Les huit sprites PNG ont été créés avec ImageGen intégré à partir des décors déjà présents dans le jeu. Les planches ont ensuite été découpées aux dimensions utilisées par la scène Godot.

## Le chapiteau, la boutique, l’oranger et le panier

La scène utilise `chapiteau.png`, `boutique_chouchou.png`, `oranger.png` et `panier_agrumes.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent 2-by-2 sprite atlas for an original Orange Town game map in a pixel-art pirate RPG. Image 1 is a style reference for its first-row props only: match the existing crisp pixel clusters, small palette, dark earthy edge and warm upper-left lighting. Fixed camera angle like the sample shop: mostly forward-facing with visible right side and some rooftop, suitable for a 2D overhead RPG. EXACTLY four separate cutout objects arranged in four strictly isolated quadrants in a square canvas, ample clear alpha margin between quadrants, every sprite fully visible. Top left: a large travelling circus big-top with two pointed peaks, red and faded ivory canvas, teal edge trim, a dark open front flap, two simple weathered tiny flags, tied ropes stay near the footprint. Top right: a cozy compact limestone pet-food shop, apricot pantile roof, dark blue wood trim, terracotta-and-navy striped awning, little carved wooden paw plaque, a low crate and sacks tucked against the front walls. Bottom left: one lush orange tree with small ripe orange fruits, deep olive-green canopy, light on its upper-left branches, gnarled thick trunk and exposed roots. Bottom right: a tiny arrangement of one wooden harvest crate with visible oranges, small dark green leaves and one orange beside it. No text, characters, people, backdrop, horizon, broad ground area, floating shadows, added objects or grid. Real fully transparent background and clear contiguous sprite silhouettes.
```

## Les trois maisons et le puits

La scène utilise `maison_abricot.png`, `maison_tuile.png`, `maison_ardoise.png` et `puits.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2x2 sprite atlas of original town props for the same top-down pixel-art pirate RPG. Image 1 is a style reference for the approved limestone corner shop, existing market stall and existing warm stone cottage. Match their front-dominant view with a little roof and right side visible, lighting upper-left and closely matched detailed pixel clusters. Keep all four props in their own quadrant with generous transparent gaps and no overlap. Top left: a simple squat one-storey honey limestone cottage, weathered apricot gabled roof, burgundy shutters and one planter, front door facing viewer. Top right: a larger slightly asymmetric two-storey sandy-ochre masonry house with warm pale copper tiles and shuttered muted green windows, broad front and a recessed wooden door, visible irregular stone base. Bottom left: compact and narrower sun-faded ochre stucco cottage with a low mottled muted slate-blue hipped roof and warm brown door, a thin line of small vines. Bottom right: low round stone village well with a small timber frame, visible dark water and one attached bucket, authentic old stone. Production assets with dark earthy outlines, matte muted warm cream and clay palette, visible chunky pixel clusters, and enough texture to feel at home alongside the reference; avoid bright flat toy colors. Each object fully isolated, no path slabs or big ground shadows, no characters, no text or props outside the individual objects. Entire background strictly transparent alpha.
```
